Capabilities

What we forge.

Five modes of dataset construction, picked and combined to match your training or evaluation objective. Every project ships with a documented schema, generation pipeline, verification layer, and eval harness.

01 / Synthesis

Pure synthetic generation

Language models, simulators, and procedural pipelines producing high-volume corpora at scale. Schema-controlled, diversity-tuned, contamination-checked.

instruction data pretraining mixes tool-use corpora
02 / Hybrid

Generation with verification

Model-generated content paired with execution checking, constraint satisfaction, or programmatic validators. Correctness guaranteed, not assumed.

verified code math reasoning structured output
03 / Trajectories

Agentic rollouts & RL data

Multi-turn agent traces in real or simulated environments, with tool calls, recovery patterns, and reward signals attached.

shopping agents coding agents browser use
04 / Expert-in-loop

Human-verified reasoning

Domain expert review, rubric grading, and preference annotation where judgment matters. Medical, legal, financial, scientific.

RLHF / DPO rubric scoring domain reasoning
05 / Adversarial

Edge cases & red-team

Deliberate construction of failure modes, jailbreak attempts, prompt injection patterns, and long-tail behaviors that natural data misses.

safety evals refusal calibration stress testing
06 / Benchmarks

Custom eval datasets

Internal benchmarks built from scratch — held-out construction, difficulty calibration, documented scoring. Signal that public benchmarks have lost.

capability evals regression suites private benchmarks
Process

From spec to delivery.

Start with a 100–500 example paid sample to evaluate fit. Scale the full dataset once the methodology clears your bar.

STEP 01

Scoping call

30 minutes to diagnose the training or eval objective, current data gaps, and verification bar. We leave with a draft spec.

STEP 02

Sample build

100–500 examples, $2K–5K, 2–3 week turnaround. Documented methodology, agreed acceptance criteria, 50% upfront.

STEP 03

Evaluation

Quality report, schema validation, and eval harness handed over. You verify against your model and your bar.

STEP 04

Full dataset

Once the sample clears, we scope and produce the full corpus at the volume and cadence you need.
